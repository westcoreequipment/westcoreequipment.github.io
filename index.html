About Westcore Equipment

Engineering Excellence Since 2012

Westcore Equipment specializes in vacuum negative pressure boiler solutions that help factories lower costs and run more efficiently. Manufacturing is based in Huzhou, Zhejiang, China, with our sales headquarters in Los Angeles, California — we combine in-house manufacturing, engineering services, and project delivery to support industrial customers in energy-intensive sectors across 18 countries.

Our portfolio of thermal energy systems, electric equipment, and specialized generators is engineered to the highest standards of efficiency, reliability, and environmental responsibility — meeting the demanding requirements of modern industrial operations in the United States and globally.
