Who We Are

Westcore Equipment — engineering vacuum boiler solutions that reduce industrial operating costs and environmental impact since 2012.

Our Story

Westcore Equipment serves customers worldwide with vacuum negative pressure boiler systems engineered for efficiency and reliability. Our manufacturing is based in Huzhou, Zhejiang, China, with our sales headquarters in Los Angeles, California. We combine in-house manufacturing, engineering services, and full project delivery to support industrial customers in energy-intensive sectors.

Founded in 2012, we have grown to serve 18 countries, completing over 450 installed projects. Our manufacturing facility produces more than 200 systems per year to meet global demand.

Our Mission

Deliver energy-efficient vacuum boiler solutions that reduce operating cost, improve safety, and support sustainable manufacturing — helping our clients remain competitive in an era of rising energy costs and tightening environmental regulations.
